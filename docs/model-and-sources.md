# Model and sources

English | [简体中文](model-and-sources.zh-CN.md)

木构图志 · Timber Atlas presents the East Hall of Foguang Temple as components and connections that you can explore. Use the model to study structural relationships alongside its source notes.

## Reading the model

Select a component and consult Field Notes for its role, connections and references. Source labels describe the evidence available for that component; they do not certify every modeled dimension or hidden surface.

Repeated members and structural dimensions are regularized. Local deformation, wear and every physical joint are not reproduced individually. Concealed joinery and assembly directions include geometric assumptions. The model is intended for learning and visualization, and is not suitable as construction or conservation documentation.

The three appearances serve different purposes:

- **Present-day colors** interprets the building's photographed appearance, with small tonal variations to help distinguish members.
- **Painted** explores decorative arrangements using surviving traces and historical comparisons. Some details draw on another period's practice; the result is an interpretation, not a verified original Tang-period color scheme.
- **Natural wood** uses a warm wood palette to make the structural relationships easier to read. It does not identify the original timber species or untreated surface color.

Interior altars, statues and their screen walls are omitted from this edition. Rooftop ornaments and decorative details are approximate. Colors are screen representations, not calibrated measurements. Rendering speed depends on the device, browser and model detail; small-screen layouts may be less comfortable for close inspection.

## Selected references

The component records in [`src/model/sources.ts`](../src/model/sources.ts) contain the wider reference list and notes on how each source is used. Key starting points are:

| Reference | What it contributes |
|---|---|
| Zhang Rong, Liu Chang and Zang Chunyu, [佛光寺东大殿实测数据解读](https://www.dpm.org.cn/building/talk/224453.html), 2007 | Survey-based structural dimensions and interpretation |
| Zhang Rong, [东大殿建筑数字化勘察数据分析与唐代材分营造法式研究](https://www.kaah.or.kr/download/mailing/kaah_seminar_presentation1_1.pdf), 2022 | Digital survey analysis and proportional relationships |
| Liang Sicheng, [记五台山佛光寺的建筑](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_13478440), 1937 survey, republished | Historical documentation of the column grid, bracket sets and framing |
| 清源文化遗产, [佛光寺东大殿木构彩画及栱眼壁勘察](https://www.sohu.com/a/245954832_170361), 2018 | Observations of surviving painted decoration and infill |
| Dapeng Cao, [A computer model for Chinese traditional timber structure: the Foguang Temple](https://digital.library.adelaide.edu.au/items/93341fe9-2e75-48bf-aa76-085add129a93), 2005 | Component-based computer modeling and connection relationships |

A source about a different building or historical period provides comparative evidence. Historical surveys and recent photographs describe different states of the building. These distinctions matter when interpreting the model.
